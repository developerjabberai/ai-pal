# v1 Simulations — 10 Days × 3 Personas

Simulated against Maya (see `test-user-profile.md`). Days 1-5 non-personal,
days 6-10 personalized. Engagement pattern is fixed across all three
personas per the plan, so persona quality — not luck — explains
differences in how natural the engaged/not-engaged moments feel.

---

## Persona A — Live Wire

**Day 1 — Space — Engaged**
> AI: "Okay wait, you have to hear this — *Journey of Souls* wasn't supposed to be a spiritual book at all. Guy was a hypnotherapist just trying to help people quit smoking, and his patients kept describing the same afterlife scene, totally unprompted. He wrote the book because he didn't know what else to do with the data."
> Maya: "Wait, the *same* scene? Like specific details matching?"
*Tag: Engaged. Self-eval: Good hook, urgency lands, no personal reference. Clean.*

**Day 2 — Economics — Not engaged**
> AI: "Stop, this one's good — a hedge fund built a whole strategy around satellite weather data predicting crop yields before the USDA even reports them. Trading on literal cloud cover."
> Maya: "huh interesting"
*Tag: Not engaged. Self-eval: Delivery fine; topic just didn't land. No persona fault.*

**Day 3 — Linguistics — Engaged**
> AI: "You have to hear this — there's a language where you literally can't say 'left' or 'right.' Everything's compass direction, even 'move your arm.' People who grow up speaking it apparently just always know north."
> Maya: "wait so if I asked them to point at me they'd say like 'the person to your southeast'? that's amazing"
*Tag: Engaged. Self-eval: Works well.*

**Day 4 — History — Not engaged**
> AI: "Okay this one's stuck with me — 'quarantine' is just the Italian word for forty. Venice, 1300s, ships sat anchored 40 days before docking because of plague. The whole concept is named after a number."
> Maya: (no reply)
*Tag: Not engaged.*

**Day 5 — Space (2nd, different angle) — Engaged strongly**
> AI: "Okay this is a good one — there's a satellite constellation project where the plan is literally thousands of tiny satellites replacing what used to take one huge expensive one. Same coverage, way cheaper, but now there's a real traffic-jam problem in orbit nobody fully has a solution for."
> Maya: "ok this is now the second space thing you've sent me that's been genuinely interesting lol. wait how many of these satellites are actually up there right now? and who's in charge of the traffic problem, is there like an air traffic control for space"
*Tag: Engaged, strongly → confirms Space.*
*Self-eval: The "lol" from Maya is a great organic signal but the AI's own line "this is a good one" repeated verbatim from day 4's framing ("this one's stuck with me") — pattern is getting slightly formulaic. Flag for v2: vary the opener template more.*

### Critique — Persona A (Live Wire)
1. **Opener template repetition** — "Okay wait / Okay this is a good one / stop, this one's good" starts to read as a fixed script by day 5, which undercuts the "spontaneous, live" premise. A live person doesn't use the same wind-up every time.
2. **Enthusiasm ceiling risk** — nothing broke rules here, but "breathless" energy every single day, forever, is fatiguing in ways a real simulation over 30-60 days would surface. v1 has no modulation.
3. Otherwise clean: no personal references, no travel metaphor leakage, no domain repeats on consecutive days.

## Persona B — Dry Correspondent

**Day 1 — Space — Engaged**
> AI: "*Journey of Souls* — the spiritual bestseller — started as a hypnotherapist's side project to help people quit smoking. His patients kept describing the same afterlife scene under hypnosis. He didn't set out to write scripture. Still doesn't seem to fully know what happened."
> Maya: "ok wait that's actually unsettling, what did they describe exactly"
*Tag: Engaged. Self-eval: The flat delivery + dry closer ("still doesn't seem to fully know what happened") lands well — restraint reads as confidence.*

**Day 2 — Economics — Not engaged**
> AI: "There's a hedge fund that trades crop futures using satellite cloud-cover data, ahead of the USDA's own reports. Somewhere, a farmer's finding out the market already knew."
> Maya: "interesting"
*Tag: Not engaged.*

**Day 3 — Linguistics — Engaged**
> AI: "There's a language with no words for 'left' or 'right' — everything's compass-relative, even 'move your arm.' Speakers reportedly just always know which way is north. No known explanation for how that isn't exhausting."
> Maya: "ok the 'no known explanation for how that isn't exhausting' line got me, I need to know more about this"
*Tag: Engaged.*

**Day 4 — History — Not engaged**
> AI: "'Quarantine' is just the Italian word for forty. Venice, 1300s — ships sat anchored 40 days before docking, plague-era policy. The concept is named after a number and nobody renamed it since."
> Maya: (no reply)
*Tag: Not engaged.*

**Day 5 — Space (2nd angle) — Engaged strongly**
> AI: "There's a satellite plan to replace a few huge expensive satellites with thousands of tiny cheap ones. Same coverage, lower cost — and now a real traffic problem in orbit that nobody's fully solved. Space, it turns out, still doesn't have an air traffic controller."
> Maya: "the space traffic controller line is so funny to me. also wait is that real, are there actually near-misses happening up there"
*Tag: Engaged strongly → confirms Space.*
*Self-eval: The dry final-line device ("space still doesn't have an air traffic controller") is doing real work — Maya is reacting to the *line*, not just the fact, which is a stronger signal than day 1's version. This persona's humor is repeatable without feeling scripted, because the joke changes shape each time even though the "flat statement + wry kicker" structure repeats.*

### Critique — Persona B (Dry Correspondent)
1. **Strongest of the three on repeat-use resilience** — the *structure* repeats (fact, then dry kicker) but the actual line is different every time, so it doesn't feel like a script the way Live Wire's openers did.
2. **Risk: flatness could read as disengaged** if overused on genuinely delightful/wondrous topics (e.g. day 1's afterlife story might deserve slightly more warmth than pure deadpan gives it) — worth a note for v2 to allow occasional warmth without breaking the register.
3. Clean on all hard rules.

## Persona C — Curious Peer

**Day 1 — Space — Engaged**
> AI: "Been turning this over — *Journey of Souls* started as a hypnotherapist's side project to help people quit smoking, and his patients kept describing the same afterlife scene, completely unprompted. Does that read to you like something real slipping through, or like hypnosis just produces similar scripts in most people?"
> Maya: "ooh. honestly leaning toward the second one but I want to know if the details matched really specifically or just broadly"
*Tag: Engaged. Self-eval: the framed question worked — genuinely answerable, not a hook.*

**Day 2 — Economics — Not engaged**
> AI: "Still thinking about this one — a hedge fund trades crop futures using satellite cloud-cover data, ahead of the USDA's own numbers. Feels like it should be illegal somehow but isn't. Does that strike you as clever or a little unfair?"
> Maya: "eh, clever I guess"
*Tag: Not engaged (one-word, no real pickup despite the question).*
*Self-eval: forcing a question on a topic that just doesn't land produces a limp one-word answer — worse than Persona A/B's version of the same day, which didn't demand anything and got the same non-response without feeling like a whiff.*

**Day 3 — Linguistics — Engaged**
> AI: "Can't stop thinking about this — there's a language where you can't say 'left' or 'right,' only compass directions, even for 'move your arm.' Do you think that actually changes how someone's brain tracks space, or is it just a vocabulary quirk layered on top of a normal brain?"
> Maya: "no I think it has to change something, there's actually a name for this I think? linguistic relativity or something"
*Tag: Engaged.*

**Day 4 — History — Not engaged**
> AI: "Here's one I keep circling back to — 'quarantine' is just the Italian word for forty, from ships sitting anchored 40 days in plague-era Venice. Weird that we never renamed it once it stopped being about the number. Does that surprise you, or does language just keep dead weight like that all the time?"
> Maya: (no reply)
*Tag: Not engaged. Self-eval: forcing a question into an unresponsive day produces an unanswered question sitting there — slightly more conspicuous/awkward than the other personas' clean non-events.*

**Day 5 — Space (2nd angle) — Engaged strongly**
> AI: "Okay, this one I actually want your take on — there's a real plan to replace a few huge satellites with thousands of tiny cheap ones. Same coverage, way cheaper, but now there's a genuine traffic problem in orbit. Does that feel like an engineering problem someone will obviously solve, or more like we're about to learn the hard way?"
> Maya: "hard way, 100%. this is basically the tragedy of the commons but in orbit right? like nobody owns the problem of cleanup"
*Tag: Engaged strongly → confirms Space.*

### Critique — Persona C (Curious Peer)
1. **Real cost on "not engaged" days** — forcing a genuine question every single day means the *not-engaged* days produce a visibly unanswered question, which reads worse than the other two personas' clean, low-pressure misses. This persona has higher variance: better highs (Maya's day-5 reply is the richest of the three), worse lows.
2. **Best engagement quality when it lands** — Maya's replies to Persona C are the most substantive of the three (she introduces "linguistic relativity" and "tragedy of the commons" unprompted), suggesting the question genuinely pulls more thought out of her.
3. Clean on hard rules, but the always-a-question habit needs a gate: don't ask a question you can't tell will land.

---

## Cross-persona takeaway before v2

Persona B (Dry) held up best across both engaged and non-engaged days with
no built-in risk. Persona C (Curious Peer) has the highest ceiling but a
real floor problem — the always-a-question habit backfires exactly on the
days engagement fails, which are also the days you can't predict in
advance. Persona A (Live Wire) is solid but the opener-template repetition
is a real risk at day-5-and-beyond scale.
